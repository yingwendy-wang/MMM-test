Visitor Distribution by Source Market

Top Source Markets

Geographic Insights

Visitors to Hong Kong come from 186 source markets, but about 65% of total visits originate from four key markets — Thailand, the Philippines, Indonesia, and the United States.

Thailand leads with the highest number of visitors, followed by the Philippines and Indonesia.

Overall, visitors mainly come from Southeast Asia and the United States, showing an uneven geographic distribution.

Although Mainland China is Hong Kong’s largest tourism source market based on official statistics, it shows limited representation in this dataset — likely due to TikTok access restrictions in mainland China.
